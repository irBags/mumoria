                       Welcome to Mumoria!

Your first task is to make a character: a gender, a race and a class.
Anytime before you have made these choices, you can interrupt with
control-C. You can also choose the race again by entering '%' at the class
question, which rolls new attributes too. Then you will be asked whether
other players may fight this character (PvP); that can change once a day.

Death is permanent, but your bank and your guild belong to your account.
In the game, ? lists the commands, and ' then /help the multiplayer ones.
